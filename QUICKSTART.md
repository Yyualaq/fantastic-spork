# Quick Start Guide

## Getting Started in 30 Seconds

1. **Open the Game**
   ```
   Open index.html in your browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Safari)
   ```

2. **Click to Play**
   - Click anywhere on the black canvas to lock mouse pointer
   - You should see your blue character, enemies, and the game world

3. **Basic Controls**
   - **WASD** = Move around
   - **Mouse** = Look around
   - **Left Click** = Attack
   - **Space** = Dodge/Roll

4. **First Steps**
   - Walk to the orange checkpoint flame and press **E**
   - This saves your progress
   - Find an enemy (gray knight) and practice combat
   - Watch your green stamina bar - you need it for all actions!

## Combat Tips

- **Light Attack**: Tap left-click for quick damage
- **Heavy Attack**: Hold left-click for 0.5s+ for massive damage
- **Dodge**: Press Space to roll - you're invincible for 0.4 seconds!
- **Lock-On**: Press Q to lock onto an enemy for easier targeting
- **Stamina**: Always keep some stamina for emergency dodges

## Objectives

1. ✓ Activate the starting checkpoint (orange flame)
2. ✓ Defeat 3 regular knight enemies
3. ✓ Find and activate the second checkpoint
4. ✓ Challenge the boss in the red arena
5. ✓ Master the art of dodge timing

## Need Help?

- **Dying?** Don't worry! You respawn at your last checkpoint
- **Out of Stamina?** Stop attacking and wait for the green bar to refill
- **Lost?** Look for the red circular arena - that's where the boss is
- **Controls Not Working?** Make sure you clicked the canvas to lock the mouse

## Pro Tips

🎯 **Lock-On** (Q key) makes combat much easier  
⚡ **Heavy attacks** deal 2x damage but cost 2x stamina  
🛡️ **Dodge timing** is everything - watch enemy wind-ups  
💚 **Stamina management** is key to survival  
🔥 **Checkpoints** restore all health and stamina
